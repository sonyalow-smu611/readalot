    Start in under two minutes

    1. Make your character

      Sign up with your email, pick a look, and tell IKDA the genres you love.

    2. Fill your shelf

      Search the library or scan a cover. Sort books into To read, Reading and Finished.

    3. Find your people

      Head to the cafe, see your reading match with each reader, and visit their rooms.

    Questions before you move in

    Is IKDA free?

    Yes. Sign up with your email and start reading. Coins are earned in the app, never bought.

    Do I need to download anything?

    No. IKDA runs in your browser and is made for your phone, so it works on any device.

    Who can see my profile?

    Only you, until you switch on “Show me on the People map” in the Hair Salon. You can switch it off any time.

    What does IKDA mean?

    It comes from 읽다 (ikda), the Korean word for “to read”.

    I only read a few books a year. Is it for me?

    Yes. Adding a book, starting it and visiting a friend all earn XP, so every bit of reading counts.
