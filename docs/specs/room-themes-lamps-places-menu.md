# Room themes, curtains and bedding, lamp switches, a barer start and a Places menu

**Status:** ready-for-agent
**Part:** Your house → My Room (owner: Sonz). The Shop's catalog is shared (see "Who has to
agree").

## Problem Statement

Every room in Readalot looks the same. When I visit Emma, Ayaan, Sofia or Noah, I see the same
peach walls, sage curtains, patchwork quilt and brass lamps as in my own room. Only a few pieces
of furniture change, so visiting doesn't feel like walking into someone else's home.

My own room also starts out so full of decorations that there's little left to make my own.
The wall is already covered in frames, shelves, hanging stars and plants. The Shop sells
furniture, but nothing that changes the room's soft furnishings, like the curtains or the bed.

The lamps look like they should switch on and off, but tapping them does nothing.

On screen, the bar of places runs along the bottom and covers the floor and the furniture
standing on it. The weather chip sits in a corner, far from the window it describes.

## Solution

- **A theme for every house.** Each reader's house has its own colour theme and look: wall and
  floor colours, curtains, bedsheet, lamps and rug. Emma's, Ayaan's, Sofia's and Noah's rooms
  are each recognisably theirs, and my room has its own starting theme.
- **Curtains and bedding in the Shop.** New Curtains and Bedding groups offer several curtain
  colours and bedsheet designs. I buy one, then hang it at the window or put it on the bed, the
  same way a reading chair takes the armchair's place. My previous set goes back to my
  inventory, and taking mine away brings back the room's own.
- **Lamp switches.** Tapping the floor lamp or the table lamp switches it on or off. A lamp that's
  on shows its lit shade, and after dark it lights its corner of the room. Switching it off at
  night lets that corner go dark.
- **A barer start.** The starting room keeps the furniture and the parts you tap, but loses most
  of the small decorations: the gallery of frames, the wall shelves and their knick-knacks, the
  hanging stars and hearts, and the extra plants and baskets. There's open wall and floor to
  furnish from the Shop.
- **A Places menu.** The bar of places becomes a labelled **Places** button with a hamburger icon
  in the top-left corner. It opens the list of places; picking one slides the room there and
  closes the list.
- **The weather chip above the window.** The weather and temperature chip sits just above the
  window and moves with the room as you look around.

## User Stories

### Themes

1. As a visitor, I want each reader's room in its own colours (walls, floor, curtains, bedsheet, lamps and rug), so that every visit feels like a different home.
2. As a visitor, I want Emma's, Ayaan's, Sofia's and Noah's rooms each to have a theme that suits them, so that the room matches the reader I met in the Cafe.
3. As a reader, I want my own room to start with its own theme (today's warm peach), so that my room is still recognisably mine.
4. As a reader, I want a theme's colours to get the same weather tint and night light as the rest of the room, so that a themed room still feels lit by the sky outside.
5. As a reader, I want furniture from the Shop to sit naturally in any theme, so that what I buy works in my room whatever its colours.
6. As a visitor, I want the still picture shown while a room loads to be a plain, neutral stand-in, so that a themed room never flashes the wrong colours for long.
7. As a reader, I want the window, clock, corkboard, bookshelf and coffee table to stay where they are in every theme, so that I always know where to tap.

### Lamp switches

26. As a reader, I want the lamps to start on, so that the room looks as it always has at night until I change it.

28. As a reader, I want tapping a lamp never to open anything else (no card, no shop), so that switching a lamp is all it does.
29. As a reader, I want the lamps to stay as I set them while I look at My Shelf and come back, so that the room keeps how I left it for the visit.
30. As a visitor, I want to switch the lamps in someone else's room for my visit, without it counting as changing their room, so that I can see their room by lamplight.

### A barer starting room

## Implementation Decisions

**Themes as data.** A house-owned themes module lists named themes. Each theme gives:

- the wall, ceiling, skirting and floor colours the room paints at runtime;
- the sprite variant for the curtains, the bedding, the floor lamp, the table lamp and the rug.

My Room uses the starting theme, today's peach. Each sample reader in the visitors module
names a theme:

| Reader | Theme | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Emma | Rose garden | Blush walls, sage curtains, floral bedding |
| Ayaan | Night sky | Slate-blue walls, navy curtains, star bedding, a brass lamp |
| Sofia | Tea room | Sage walls, cream lace curtains, gingham bedding |
| Noah | Reading den | Deep green walls, mustard curtains, tartan bedding, a green glass lamp |

The room component takes the theme as a prop. The painter reads wall and floor colours from the
theme rather than from the fixed palette, and swaps in the theme's variant sprites. Tints and
night light work as today.

**Variant sprites from the art script.** Each curtain colour, bedding design, lamp style
(unlit and lit) and rug is its own sprite. The room's art script draws them with the existing
curtain, bed, lamp and rug drawing functions, now taking colours as parameters. All variants are
loaded with the room, so a theme or a new bedsheet never pops in.

**Curtains and bedding are furniture with one spot each.** The furniture library gains two
groups:

- **Curtains,** surface `curtains`: one spot at the window, and the piece replaces both curtain
  pieces.
- **Bedding,** surface `bed`: one spot on the bed, and the piece replaces the bed's sprite with
  the same bed in that bedding.

These zones behave like the reading nook:

- **One at a time:** placing a second piece sends the first back to the inventory.
- **Taking it away:** brings back the theme's own.
- **The catalog's rules:** the "fits this zone" and "swap the one already there" rules,
  generalised from the reading nook to these zones.

The Shop shows the new groups after the existing ones, with pixel thumbnails (the curtain or a
folded sheet). Each item's blurb is "At the window" or "On the bed". Expected prices are
40–70 credits.

**Lamps.**

- **Buttons:** the floor lamp and the table lamp become labelled toggle buttons over their
  sprites, named "Floor lamp" and "Table lamp" with a pressed state.
- **State:** each lamp's on/off state lives for the visit in the room's page state, like placed
  furniture. Both start on.
- **Painting:** the painter takes the lamp states. A lamp that's on uses its lit sprite day and
  night. At night only lamps that are on add their pool of light (and their bloom). The window's
  light and the fairy lights are unchanged.
- **Placement:** the lamp buttons sit under the room's other buttons, the same way placed
  furniture's do, so they never block the bookshelf or the book on the coffee table.

**Barer starting layout.**

- **Removed from the room's piece list:** the gallery frames and wall shelves (with their jars,
  candles, photos and book stacks), the hanging decorations, the bed-wall posters and frames, the
  laundry basket, the knit basket, the slippers, the floor book piles, and all but one plant.
- **Kept:** the bunting, window, curtains, window seat, pillows, floor lamp, armchair, throw,
  side table, rug, coffee table with the open book and mug, bookshelf, clock, corkboard, light
  switch, dresser (with its radio), mirror, nightstand and table lamp, and the bed.
- **Kept as is:** the spots for Shop furniture, and the parts of the room (the places).
- **The still picture:** shown while the room loads; regenerated to match.

**The Places menu.**

- **Button:** the bar of places is replaced by a button with a hamburger icon and the visible
  label "Places", in the room's top-left corner, at the same height as the credits chip on the
  right.
- **The list:** a small popover below the button, named "Places in the room", with one button
  per place. It behaves like a disclosure: `aria-expanded` on the button, Esc and a tap outside
  close it, and focus returns to the button.
- **Choosing a place:** slides the room there, as the bar did, then closes the list.
- **Before the room has loaded:** the Bookshelf, Chair and Corkboard entries open what they open
  today.
- **The room's sizing:** the space kept clear along the bottom for the bar is now only enough for
  the round room menu or Leave a note button.

**The weather chip.** The chip moves inside the room's stage and is positioned in art
coordinates, centred on the window and just above the curtain rod (clear of the bunting). It
scrolls with the room and keeps its pixel icon and label. On a laptop wide enough that the room
is centred, it's still above the window.

**Who has to agree:** the Shop's catalog (the shared decoration store) gets the new groups and
the generalised one-at-a-time zones. That's shared, so it needs a teammate's review. Everything
else is in the house folder and its art script.

## Testing Decisions

- **One seam:** Playwright journeys through the real app, as for every house feature. Tests use
  labelled buttons, region names and visible text, and canvas pixels only where the change is in
  the picture alone. The furniture tests already do this with their colour-under-a-button helper.
  Tests never import themes, layouts or the store.
- **What a good test is here:** it checks what a reader sees or can do. For example, the wall
  behind Emma's bookshelf is a different colour from mine. It doesn't check which theme object
  was used.
- **Prior art:**
  - `furniture.spec.js`: Shop groups, buying and placing, chair swapping, canvas colour checks by
    day and night.
  - `visit-room.spec.js`: visiting a reader's room.
  - `room.spec.js`: places, the weather chip, the room at night.
  - `house-desktop.spec.js`: laptop sizing.
- **New and updated checks:**
  - **Themes:** a bare stretch of wall in each reader's room and in mine is a different colour;
    each reader's curtains and bed differ from mine.
  - **Shop:** it has Curtains and Bedding groups with five or more pieces each. Hanging curtains
    changes the colour at the window. A second set swaps the first back to the inventory. Taking
    them away restores the theme's. Bedding is checked the same way at the bed.
  - **Lamps:** each lamp is a button that toggles its pressed state. Its shade changes colour
    when switched. At night the wall near a switched-off lamp is darker than with it on. Lamps
    keep their state after a trip to My Shelf.
  - **Barer start:** the wall where the gallery of frames used to hang is now plain wall colour,
    and every tappable part of the room is still there.
  - **Places:** the menu opens, closes (by Esc, a tap outside, and picking a place) and slides
    the room. The existing tests' "turn to a place" helpers open it first.
  - **Weather chip:** it sits above the window's glass, horizontally within the window, and moves
    when the room scrolls.
- **Done when:** the client build passes and the full e2e suite passes.

## Out of Scope

- **Choosing a whole theme for my own room** (wallpaper or paint in the Shop). Only curtains and
  bedding are for sale; my room's base theme stays the starting one.
- **Saving lamp states, curtains or bedding across reloads or devices.** They last for the visit,
  as placed furniture does today.
- **Visitors changing anything that lasts** in someone else's room. Their lamp switching is for
  their visit only.
- **New furniture beyond curtains and bedding,** and changes to the Shop's prices for existing
  items.
- **Moving or resizing the room's large furniture,** or new parts of the room for the Places
  menu.
